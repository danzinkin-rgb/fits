How to play

  1. One rule is the secret one. You see a shortlist of possible rules. Some numbers, words or pictures fit the secret rule. Others don't.
  2. Test anything. Type a number or a real word, or tap a picture, and see which side it lands on. Tap rules to cross off the ones that can't be right.
  3. Name the rule. You have six moves. Each test is a move, and so is each guess at the rule.
12407 Even numbers, or multiples of 4? Test 6: it splits them.

The fewer moves, the better your score. A new rule arrives every midnight, and they get harder through the week.
