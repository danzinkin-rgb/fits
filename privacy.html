Privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026

What the game stores

Fits keeps your progress, streak, statistics and settings in your browser's local storage, on your device only. We do not have accounts, and none of this is sent to us. Clearing your browser data for this site removes it.

What we collect

We collect anonymous information about how the game is played, so we can make it better: which puzzle was played, the moves made, the result, how long it took, and whether features such as tidy up were used. If you rate the game, we receive your star rating and any comment you type.

This information is not linked to you. We do not use cookies for it, and we do not keep any identifier on your device: each visit gets a random code that is forgotten when you close the page. The data is processed for us by PostHog on servers in the EU. Like any web service, PostHog sees your IP address when the data arrives, and uses it to work out an approximate country.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load the page, as any web host does. See GitHub's privacy statement for how they handle that.

Advertising

Fits may show adverts provided by Google AdSense. When adverts are switched on, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to show and measure ads, and to personalise them if you agree. Visitors in the UK and Europe are asked for consent before any personalised advertising. You can change how Google personalises ads at adssettings.google.com.

Children

Fits is a general-audience puzzle and is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Use "Rate Fits" in the game's menu and leave a comment.
